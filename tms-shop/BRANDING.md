# TMS visual identity

**Brand line:** Made for your people.

TMS makes custom apparel for brands, events and communities. The identity should feel considered, confident and communal. Use direct language about the product and process; avoid claims about customers, production capacity or quality guarantees without evidence.

## Visual language

- Ink black and warm off-white form the base. Olive is the primary action colour; pale sage highlights the hero headline and primary hero action.
- Keep the existing Big Shoulders Display headings and Archivo body text. Oversized display headings pair with small, widely spaced section labels.
- The typographic TMS wordmark includes a compact “THE MERCH SHOP” lockup and a small asterisk motif, repeated in the brand strip.
- Photography leads. Use generous image areas, quiet borders and 4px corners. Replace prototype images with approved TMS photography before launch.
- Copy should speak to belonging: “Made for your people”, “Pick your canvas”, and “You bring the vision. We make it wearable.”

## Implementation

`branding.css` contains the visual refinement layered over the prototype's base styles. Existing light-theme support is retained; the hero remains ink black in either theme. Mobile switches the split hero to a stacked layout and the collection to two columns.

Checkout and payments remain a demonstration. No production payment integration is included in this design work.
